SmartTeX options

Configure where SmartTeX enhances your LaTeX editor.

Define domains where SmartTeX should be active. Enter one hostname or URL per line. Wildcard subdomains are supported; for example, *.example.org includes the main domain and its subdomains. URL paths and trailing slashes are ignored. Lines beginning with # are ignored.

Features

Choose which SmartTeX previews should appear in document editors.

Live previews

Move the text cursor into an inline or display equation to show a live KaTeX preview beside it. SmartTeX applies \newcommand, \renewcommand, and \providecommand definitions found earlier in the active LaTeX file. Moving into a tabular, tabularx, longtable, or array block shows a live table preview with its rules, alignment, formatted text, and inline mathematics. Moving into a figure block shows its project image or PDF together with its caption.
